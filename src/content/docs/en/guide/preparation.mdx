First, complete the following preparation steps.

## 1. Create a Claude Platform Account

<Steps>

1. Go to [platform.claude.com](https://platform.claude.com) and create an account. You can sign up with a Google account or an email address.

    ![](../../guide/images/2026-07-04-03-29-32.png)

2. Enter your name and other details.

    ![](../../guide/images/2026-07-04-05-51-41.png)

3. Select "Skip for now".

    ![](../../guide/images/2026-07-04-05-51-50.png)

4. Your account has been created. After signing in you land on the dashboard. The left sidebar is split into two groups: "Build & configure" (Messages, Agents, Resources) and "Organization" (Spend, Keys & access, Security & data, Settings).

    ![](../../guide/images/2026-10-27-dashboard.jpg)

5. You can change the display language from "Language" in the menu that opens when you click your account name at the bottom left.

    ![](../../guide/images/2026-10-27-language.jpg)

</Steps>

<Aside type="note" title="This is separate from your claude.ai account">
A subscription account for the chat service [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) (including Pro/Max plans) cannot be used to access the API. You need a **Claude Platform** account, which is for developers.
</Aside>

## 2. How to Add API Credits

Managed Agents is used via the API, so you need API credits (a prepaid usage balance).

<Aside type="tip">
API credits will be distributed at the event. Purchasing them in advance is not required.
</Aside>

<Tabs>

<TabItem label="Purchase credits">

<Steps>

1. Click the balance shown at the top right of the screen (for example `$0.00`), or open "Spend" → "Billing & payments" from the sidebar.

    ![](../../guide/images/2026-10-27-spending.jpg)

1. Click "Buy credits" under "Credit balance", register a credit card, and purchase credits.

    ![](../../guide/images/2026-10-27-billing.jpg)

</Steps>

</TabItem>

<TabItem label="Use workshop credits">

<Steps>

1. Make sure you are signed in to the Claude Platform.

1. Open the URL that is shared during the workshop.

1. Check that the email address and organization name are correct, and enter your First name and Last name. Under "Which Anthropic products do you currently use?", check "Claude API", then click "Submit request".

    ![](../../guide/images/2026-08-10-02-43-40.png)

1. A confirmation screen appears. Your request is reviewed, and you will get an email once the credits are granted.

    ![](../../guide/images/2026-08-10-02-44-08.png)

</Steps>

</TabItem>

</Tabs>

<Aside type="tip" title="Estimated cost for the hands-on">
Running through the entire workshop costs **roughly $5–10** (this varies depending on how much trial and error you do and the nature of the tasks). Agents generated from the "Create" screen in the beginner section use `claude-opus-5-5` by default, so a single test can cost $1 to several dollars (each test session is capped at $5). The material also shows how to switch the model to `claude-sonnet-5`. The intermediate section mainly uses `claude-haiku-4-5`, and the advanced section `claude-sonnet-5`.
</Aside>


## 3. Set a Spending Limit

To prevent unexpected charges, set a spending limit.

<Steps>

1. Open "Spend" → "Spend limits" from the sidebar.

1. Click "Adjust limit" under "Monthly spend limit" and set the limit.

    ![](../../guide/images/2026-10-27-spend-limits.jpg)

</Steps>


## 4. Create a Workspace

Create a workspace.

<Steps>

1. Open "Settings" → "Workspaces" from the sidebar and click "Create workspace".

    ![](../../guide/images/2026-10-27-workspaces.jpg)

1. Enter "workshop" as the name, pick a color, and click "Create workspace".

    ![](../../guide/images/2026-10-27-workspace-new.jpg)

</Steps>

<Aside>
You switch workspaces with the workspace selector (the part showing `Default`) at the top of the submenu that appears when you open a menu such as "Agents". You can also create a workspace from there. On the following pages, make sure this selector shows "workshop" before you start.

![](../../guide/images/2026-10-27-workspace-selector.jpg)

</Aside>


## 5. Prepare an Editor

From the intermediate section onward, you will edit code and configuration files. Any editor you normally use is fine. If you have no particular preference, we recommend [Visual Studio Code](https://code.visualstudio.com/).
